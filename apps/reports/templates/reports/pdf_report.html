WHO

Tawag Paglaum Centro Bisaya is a government-run program by the Department of Health - CHD 7 and Vicente Sotto Memorial Medical Center under the Psychiatry Department, with the goal of providing intervention for people having emotional crisis, particularly with suicidal risk.

Tawag Paglaum operates through a call-based service with lines open 24/7 from Monday to Sunday including holidays. Responders attending the calls are mental health professionals who are adept in assisting the callers in any way possible.

Tawag Paglaum was launched last August 2015 formerly known as “Hopeline” under the funding of Natasha Goulbourn Foundation due to unforeseen events Hopeline was eventually adopted by VSMMC in 2017 and was renamed as “Tawag Paglaum-Centro Bisaya”.
